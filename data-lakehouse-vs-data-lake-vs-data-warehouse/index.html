Lakehouse vs Lake vs Warehouse

The evolution of enterprise data architecture.

The Evolution

To understand the data lakehouse, you must understand the two architectures it supersedes.

Data Warehouse

Optimized for structured data and BI workloads. High performance, strict schemas, but can be expensive and inflexible for unstructured data (like images or text) needed for modern AI.

Data Lake

A repository for raw, unstructured data. Highly scalable and cost-effective, but suffers from data swamp issues: poor governance, lack of ACID transactions, and slow query performance.

Data Lakehouse

Combines the best of both. Uses low-cost object storage (like a lake) but layers open table formats (like Iceberg) on top to provide schema enforcement, transactions, and performance (like a warehouse).
