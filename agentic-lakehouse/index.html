What Is an Agentic Lakehouse?

The governed data foundation for autonomous AI agents.

The Rise of AI Agents

As organizations deploy LLMs and autonomous agents to take actions, they need an architecture that provides context, governed execution, and open standards. The Agentic Lakehouse evolves the traditional Data Lakehouse to serve AI systems directly.

Core Requirements

  • Context & Semantic Meaning: Agents need to understand what data means, not just where it lives.
  • Governed Execution & Trust: Robust RBAC and row-level security ensuring agents only see what they are authorized to see.
  • Multicloud & Open Standards: Preventing vendor lock-in as AI workloads shift across clouds.
